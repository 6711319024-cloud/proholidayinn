FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQ)

When is check-in?

Check-in time is 3:00 PM.

When is check-out?

Check-out time is 12:00 PM (noon).

When can I use the Wi-Fi?

Wi-Fi can be used after check-in. Network: HolidayInn. Username: your last name. Password: your room number.

Where is breakfast?

Breakfast is at Charm Thai Restaurant from 6:30 AM to 10:30 AM.

What is the difference between Main Wing and Busakorn Wing?

Main Wing focuses on family activities, Kids Club, Splash Zone, and central pools. Busakorn Premium Wing offers a tranquil Thai-inspired ambiance with exclusive pools and quiet areas.
